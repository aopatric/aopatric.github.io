Hi there! I'm Angel, and I recently graduated from MIT (May 2026) with a B.S. in AI and Decision Making. I'm currently enrolled in the MEng program at MIT, and am using this Fall 2026 semester to work on independent research projects before returning to complete my thesis. At the moment, I'm working on two projects in particular, namely a revist of [my work](https://github.com/aopatric/post-training-EoS) on the Edge of Stability phenomena for language model post-training and a [reward hacking project](https://github.com/aopatric/reward-hacking-study) studying how we can use probing methods to distinguish hacked solves form legitimate ones to inform active intervention methods for model alignment.

My time as an undergrad was spent across a few different research groups across MIT. I was fortunate enough to help design and prototype a benchmark for spatial reasoning in both neurosymbolic and language-based models built around the inverse origami problem (later published by the lab as [OrigamiBench](https://icml.cc/virtual/2026/68020)) with Prof. Armando Solar-Lezama's group at CSAIL, contribute core training and communication infrastructure to a decentralized and data-private solution for federated learning ([SONAR Web](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3737899.3768523), ACM FLEdge-AI '25, Best Presentation) with the Camera Culture group at the Media Lab, and adapt masked language modeling approaches to single-cell transcriptomics data from the CELLxGENE Census for imputation and classification with the Edelman Lab at IMES.

Outside of my MIT-affiliated work, my personal research interests lie heavily in the alignment of highly capable models, from the very first training step. Aside from my reward hacking study, I have much interest in the existing literature on sparse autoencoders and activation patching, as thsese methods seem most closely aligned with my end goal for the scalable, performant, capable, and most importantly safe models of the future.

When I'm not working on one of the above things, I'm often doing any one of producing or DJing or consuming music, poking around with the latest AI coding tools, or just hanging out with my cat, Cillian. I'm also a submission grappling fan and have been training for a little over a year now. I'm always open to dialogue and I deeply enjoy discussing research topics, so feel free to reach out!
